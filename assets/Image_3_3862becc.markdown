## Brand & Style

This design system channels the quiet reverence and monumental stillness of untamed nocturnal wilderness. It evokes the feeling of gazing into an Arctic fjord beneath an aurora borealis or resting within an obsidian alpine shelter during twilight. The aesthetic is museum-grade, contemplative, and editorial—balancing raw geological gravitas with sublime luminous atmosphere.

The design movement combines **Atmospheric Dark Glassmorphism** with **High-End Editorial Minimalism**:
- **Atmospheric Depth:** Deep obsidian voids punctuated by low-frequency diffuse ambient glows (aurora teal, ethereal emerald, canyon amber).
- **Museum Curation:** Generous margins, fine architectural strokes, and contemplative pacing that prioritize photography and high-fidelity artifacts.
- **Sculpted Glass:** Ultra-refined translucent plates with micro-edge refraction, treating interface elements like precision-milled dark crystal lenses rather than plastic overlays.

## Colors

The palette is anchored in abyssal earth tones and illuminated by ethereal atmospheric phenomenon.

### Foundation & Surfaces
- **Obsidian Void (`#070a11`):** The foundational substrate; absolute deep ground.
- **Midnight Navy (`#0a0e17`):** Elevated canvas background for staggered depth and subtle sectional separation.
- **Glass Slate Surface (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.55)`):** Base fill for interactive panels and elevated card containers.
- **Translucent Edge Stroke (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`):** Hairline boundary defining frosted panels against deep backgrounds.

### Celestial Accents
- **Primary / Ethereal Emerald (`#10b981`):** Primary focal accents, active states, and focal navigational items. Conveys primordial forest and glacial mineral deposits.
- **Secondary / Aurora Teal (`#2dd4bf`):** Radiance markers, interactive hovers, and high-frequency light ribbons.
- **Alpine Frost Blue (`#93c5fd`):** Cool atmospheric highlights, metadata highlights, and secondary informative chips.
- **Tertiary / Canyon Amber (`#f59e0b`):** Geologic warmth, badges, premium tiers, and rare specimen tags.

### Typography & Content Shades
- **Luminescent Pure (`#f8fafc`):** High-impact display headlines and pivotal stats.
- **Glacial Mist (`#94a3b8`):** Editorial body copy, labels, and secondary context.
- **Deep Lichen (`#475569`):** Inactive indicators, structural borders, and de-emphasized metadata.

## Typography

The typographical hierarchy creates an intentional dialogue between classic editorial grace and hyper-crisp navigational precision.

- **Editorial Titles (`Playfair Display`):** Reserved for hero narrative sweeps, exhibition titles, and section headlines. It gives large-scale visual weight reminiscent of high-end art monographs. Use regular (`400`) to medium (`500`) weights with tight letter-spacing to enhance exclusivity.
- **Structural UI & Metadata (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Selected for its pristine clarity at low opacity and small scales on dark backgrounds. It ensures that geographic coordinates, telemetry, elevation markers, and expository text remain effortless to read.
- **Curatorial Tracking:** All metadata titles and categorizations (`label-caps`) must use uppercase transformations with generous character spacing (`0.14em`) to mirror archival exhibition plates.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an expansive, cinematic 12-column responsive fluid grid that prioritizes panoramic imagery and high-contrast breathing room.

### Rhythm & Proportions
- **Canvas Boundaries:** Desktop screens use generous outer margins (`3rem` / `48px`) to detach UI modules from the screen edges, evoking a framed gallery experience.
- **Vertical Airflow:** Vertical separation between showcase units relies on large modular increments (`space-xl` and above) to avoid visual crowding and let full-bleed landscape assets command attention.
- **Responsive Adaptations:**
  - **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns, `1.5rem` gutters, and `3rem` margins. Complex dual-pane views pairing full-height media with floating glass inspect panels.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, `1.25rem` gutters, `2rem` margins. Translucent sidebars condense into floating dock systems.
  - **Mobile (<768px):** 4 columns, `1rem` gutters, `1.25rem` margins. Multi-column editorial cards stack vertically into seamless continuous streams.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed through luminous translucency, backdrop filtration, and diffuse chromatic halos instead of harsh drop shadows.

### Glassmorphic Layers
- **Tier 0 (Abyssal Floor):** Pure `#070a11` or `#0a0e17` with subtle, organic SVG noise (opacity 0.03) to prevent digital flatness.
- **Tier 1 (Atmospheric Base):** Backdrop-filter `blur(16px)` with surface `rgba(10, 14, 23, 0.65)` and a subtle 1px border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`. Used for content carousels, secondary panels, and navigation bars.
- **Tier 2 (Elevated Focal Cards):** Backdrop-filter `blur(24px)` with surface `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.5)` bounded by a gradient border: `linear-gradient(135deg, rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.16) 0%, rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.02) 100%)`.

### Luminous Halos
- **Emerald Aurora Bloom:** `0px 20px 60px -15px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.15)` coupled with a low-intensity inner highlight `inset 0px 1px 1px 0px rgba(45, 212, 191, 0.25)`.
- **Canyon Warmth Accent:** `0px 16px 48px -12px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.14)` for highlighted artifacts or seasonal showcases.
- **Directional Refraction:** All active glass components feature an imperceptible top-edge internal stroke (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`) that mimics physical light hitting the bevel of dark crystal.

## Shapes

The design system employs a refined geometric curve vocabulary. An outer radius factor of `2` (`0.5rem` / `8px` base, scaling to `1rem` / `16px` for structural cards and `1.5rem` / `24px` for focal hero containers) creates architectural discipline without brutal sharpness.

- **Standard Cards & Modals:** `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / `16px`) to balance optical softness with editorial framing.
- **Interactive Controls (Inputs, Buttons):** `rounded` (`0.5rem` / `8px`) provides tactile certainty.
- **Pills & Status Nodes:** Full rounded radii (`9999px`) are reserved exclusively for tags, telemetry chips, and filter toggles to distinguish functional micro-controls from structural content architecture.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Aurora Glow:** Emerald surface (`#10b981`) with deep obsidian text (`#070a11`), font weight `600`, accompanied by an ambient emerald glow blur. On hover, background shifts toward aurora teal (`#2dd4bf`) with an increased radial spread.
- **Translucent Glass Button:** Background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, text `#f8fafc`. On hover, background transitions to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` and border brightens to `rgba(45, 212, 191, 0.4)`.
- **Text / Curatorial Link:** Upper-case `label-caps` in `#93c5fd` with a micro line hover animation extending from center to edges.

### Cards & Showcase Panels
- **Landscape Media Card:** Frosted dark base with deep clipping radius. Image anchors the top or fills the background with a multi-stop bottom gradient (`rgba(7, 10, 17, 0) 0%, rgba(7, 10, 17, 0.85) 75%, #070a11 100%`).
- **Edge Detailing:** Cards feature a `1px` translucent boundary with a subtle `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Coordinates & Telemetry:** Monospaced styling using `numeric-data`, encased in `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.8)` with hairline borders and an aurora teal dot indicator (`6px` circular badge with pulsing glow).
- **Taxonomy Pill:** Capsule form with `label-caps`, neutral dark background, and an alpine frost or canyon amber accent point.

### Form Inputs & Fields
- **Surface:** `rgba(10, 14, 23, 0.7)` with an inset border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`. Placeholder in glacial mist (`#94a3b8`).
- **Focus State:** 1px boundary shifts to emerald glow (`#10b981`) with an outer atmospheric bloom `0 0 16px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.2)`.

### Lists & Archival Catalogs
- **Divider Lines:** 1px gradient lines fading from transparent at the screen edge to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` in the center.
- **Hover Rows:** Subtle surface shift to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.02)` with coordinate markers sliding 4px horizontally.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Framework:** `0.5rem` rounded square for checkboxes, full circle for radios.
- **Unchecked:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)` fill with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)` border.
- **Checked:** Solid emerald (`#10b981`) with an obsidian glyph and micro teal glow halo.
